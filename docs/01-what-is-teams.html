What SLATE Teams is

SLATE Teams runs on your own machine. No server, nothing uploaded, model keys stay local. It makes one thing easy: several people and a crew of AI working at the same table — with a trace left behind for every decision.

The wordmark is 席, which means both a seat at the table and the act of taking one. The interface follows the "Ledger" identity: graphite paper, red-ink annotations, square corners, hairline rules like a ledger page.

#The three things it is for

WhereWhat it ownsRead
Debate hallPeople and AI seats in one discussion; the host advances rounds; conclusions need a signatureTeam
OfficeAI staff pick up work on their shift, every delivery passes a review, cost lands per employeeOffice
LedgerWho said it, who approved it, how many tokens — stored, exportable, restorableProjects & data
In one line

The debate hall is collaboration at the same moment — everyone in one discussion. The office is collaboration at different moments — you leave the work, an AI staff member does it on shift, you check it when you're back.

#Six top-level tabs

  • Chat — the ordinary one-to-one conversation: tool calls, approvals, context budget.
  • Team — the debate hall. Seats, the feed, and "waiting for your signature" in three columns.
  • Office — AI staff roster, duty schedule, hand-offs between desks, the delivery queue.
  • Board — spread a discussion into a reasoning chain and workflow nodes.
  • Extensions — seven columns: skills, tools, reply modes, evolved tools, MCP, expert packs, Actions. What the model is allowed to use is decided here.
  • Settings — models, theme, language, data & privacy, and each token-spending AI feature.

#How it relates to SLATE

SLATE Teams is a sibling of SLATE. They share the base — chat, tools, skills, board, data layer — and differ in the collaboration layer: multi-seat debates, tickets with narrowed scope, AI staff on a duty roster.

Both install on one machine: ports, data directories, installer identity and update feeds are separate, so neither overwrites the other and no configuration is shared.

Which one first

If it's only you, SLATE is lighter. If you want people in the same discussion, or AI working by shift, use SLATE Teams.

#Where your data is

Every conversation, seat, job, message and ledger entry lives in data/ inside the install directory as local SQLite files. Offline means no requests; the network is used only when you call a model API or check for updates.

How to back it up — and why the two "clear" buttons are not the same thing — is in Projects & data.
